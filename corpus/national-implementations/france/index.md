# France — AI Act transposition status & national AI authority

## Fetch log (2026-08-25)
- https://cnil.fr/fr/intelligence-artificielle → HTTP 200 (CNIL AI hub page reachable).

## Known / reported (TO BE VERIFIED)
- CNIL operates an AI department and is widely reported as France's intended data-protection
  component of AI oversight; press reports indicate France intends a split between CNIL and
  another authority for market surveillance (candidate often named: ARCOM or DGCCRF). Unconfirmed.
- No standalone transposition act needed (Regulation); French implementation concerns authority
  designation only.

## Update (checked 2026-08-29, web search — cnil.fr, perinove.fr, leto.legal)
CNIL was **officially designated** France's primary AI Act regulator: on 12 Feb 2026 the
government tabled amendment n°442 to the EU-law-adaptation bill, amending the
Informatique-et-Libertés Act to give CNIL implementation of the AI Act in national law; reported
as formally confirmed around early March 2026 (Perinove, 3 Mar 2026: "la CNIL officiellement
désignée régulateur principal"). France still uses a split-governance model — other sectoral
bodies (DGCCRF, ARCOM) retain roles for specific domains — but CNIL is now the lead/reference
authority, no longer just a "candidate". Source: https://www.cnil.fr/fr/entree-en-vigueur-du-reglement-europeen-sur-lia-les-premieres-questions-reponses-de-la-cnil ,
https://perinove.fr/2026/03/03/ia-act-la-cnil-officiellement-designee-regulateur-principal-en-france/ .
Not independently verified against Legifrance/JORF primary text.
