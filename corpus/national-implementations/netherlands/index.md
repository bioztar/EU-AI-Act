# Netherlands — AI Act transposition status & national AI authority

## Known / reported (TO BE VERIFIED)
- Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch DPA) hosts an AI-regulation team and is reported as the
  designated coordinating AI supervisory authority; Dutch government announced AP as AI Act
  regulator (2024). Additional sectoral authorities expected.
- No transposition act required (Regulation).

## Update (checked 2026-08-29, web search — twobirds.com, Loyens & Loeff, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl)
Still in progress, not yet finalised. On **20 April 2026** the Dutch government published a
**draft** Implementation Act setting out the national AI-supervision governance structure — laid
before the House of Representatives but not reported as enacted as of 2026-08-29. Model is
decentralised: **AP (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)** and **RDI (Rijksinspectie Digitale
Infrastructuur)** jointly coordinate, with up to **8 sectoral/domain market-surveillance
authorities** doing the actual supervision — a more fragmented picture than "AP coordinating"
alone. Not independently checked against the bill text or Staatsblad.
