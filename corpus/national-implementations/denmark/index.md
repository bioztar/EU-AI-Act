# Denmark — AI Act transposition status & national AI authority

## Fetch log (2026-08-25)
- https://www.datatilsynet.dk/ → HTTP 200 (Datatilsynet site reachable).

## Known / reported (TO BE VERIFIED)
- Datatilsynet (Danish Data Protection Authority) has been reported as Denmark's intended
  main AI supervisory authority; Danish government announced its AI authority model in 2024-2025.
- No transposition act required (Regulation).

## Update (checked 2026-08-29, web search — digitalpolicyalert.org, ai-regulation.com, regulations.ai)
Confirms and refines the 2026-08-25 report: **Law No. 467 of 14 May 2025** ("Lov om supplerende
bestemmelser til forordningen om kunstig intelligens") is in force since 2 Aug 2025 and
designates **Digitaliseringsstyrelsen** (Danish Agency for Digitisation) — not Datatilsynet — as
the lead notifying authority/single point of contact. Datatilsynet and Domstolsstyrelsen are
additional named market-surveillance authorities, with Datatilsynet specifically covering AI
systems that process personal data. So Datatilsynet is a market-surveillance authority, but the
coordinating/lead role sits with Digitaliseringsstyrelsen, contrary to the "Datatilsynet as main
authority" framing previously carried here. Not independently checked against Retsinformation.dk.
