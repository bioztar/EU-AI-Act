## Update (checked 2026-08-29, web search — bundesnetzagentur.de, bmds.bund.de, bundestag.de)
**Enacted.** The debated proposal became the **KI-Marktüberwachungs- und
Innovationsförderungsgesetz (KI-MIG)** — "Gesetz zur Durchführung der Verordnung (EU) 2024/1689"
— passed by the Bundestag and **in force since 29 July 2026**. It designates the
**Bundesnetzagentur (BNetzA)** as Germany's central AI market-surveillance authority, contact
point, and complaints office, with a coordination/competence centre ("Koordinierungs- und
Kompetenzzentrum") housed there — not the previously-reported BSI+BfDI "KI-Verbund" network
structure. BNetzA also runs a KI-Service-Desk and AI regulatory sandboxes ("KI-Reallabore").
Sources: https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/1112336 ,
https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/Digitales/KI/14_Marktueberwachung/start.html ,
https://bmds.bund.de/aktuelles/pressemitteilungen/detail/neues-ki-gesetz-tritt-in-kraft .
Not independently re-verified against the Bundesgesetzblatt text itself — treat the enactment
date and BNetzA designation as reported-and-cross-confirmed (official BNetzA + BMDS pages agree)
rather than primary-legal-text-checked.
