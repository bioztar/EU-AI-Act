# Ireland — AI Act transposition status & national AI authority

## Fetch log (2026-08-25)

## Known / reported (TO BE VERIFIED)
- Data Protection Commission (DPC) reported as Ireland's lead AI supervisory authority;
  government statements (2024-2025) indicated DPC would take the AI Act coordination role.
- No transposition act required (Regulation).

## Update (checked 2026-08-29, web search — betterregulation.com, Matheson, regulations.ai)
Superseded by enacted legislation. The **Regulation of Artificial Intelligence Act 2026** was
signed into law by President Connolly on **21 July 2026**. It creates a new independent
**AI Office of Ireland** as national coordinator/single point of contact, while **13 sectoral
bodies were designated as market-surveillance authorities** via Statutory Instrument No.
366/2025 — including the Data Protection Commission (DPC), Central Bank of Ireland, ComReg, and
the CCPC — not DPC alone as previously reported. The AI Office is expected operational from
2 Aug 2026, with full compulsory information-gathering powers from December 2026. Not
independently checked against the enacted Act text or the SI itself.
