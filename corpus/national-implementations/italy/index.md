# Italy — AI Act transposition status & national AI authority

## Fetch log (2026-08-25)
- https://www.agid.gov.it/ → HTTP 200 (site reachable; content not parsed in this pass).

## Known / reported (TO BE VERIFIED)
- Law No. 132/2025 (Legge 132/2025), published ~25 September 2025 — Italy's omnibus AI law,
  among the first comprehensive national AI framework laws in the EU; designates AgID
  (Agenzia per l'Italia Digitale) as notifying authority and ACN (Agenzia per la Cybersicurezza
  Nazionale) as AI market-surveillance authority.
- The AI Act itself applies directly; L.132/2025 covers complementary national provisions.

## Update (checked 2026-08-29, web search — OECD.AI, Cleary Gottlieb, noze.it)
Confirms the 2026-08-25 finding and adds detail: L.132/2025 in force since 10 Oct 2025;
AgID = notifying authority, ACN = market-surveillance authority and single EU point of contact
(unchanged). New since fetch date: on **10 June 2026** the Council of Ministers gave preliminary
approval to two implementing legislative decrees (governance, training, biometrics, liability);
final adoption expected within the October 2026 delegation deadline — i.e. still in progress, not
finalised, as of 2026-08-29.
