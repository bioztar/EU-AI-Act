## Mandate context (stable facts)
- The Commission requested European standardisation organisations (via Reg (EU) No 1025/2012)
  to draft harmonised standards supporting AI Act conformity (mandate M/587, 2023).
- CEN-CENELEC Joint Technical Committee 21 ("JTC 21") develops them; ISO/IEC JTC 1 work is
  adopted in parallel where possible.
- Harmonised standards are voluntary; conformance creates presumption of conformity with the
  corresponding AI Act requirements (Art. 40 of the Act, referencing Reg 1025/2012).

## Timeline notes (reported)
- First batches of drafts targeted for public enquiry ~2025; final harmonised standards expected
  through 2026–2027, with publication of references in the Official Journal following.
- Annex III high-risk areas each get sector-specific guidance layers over time.
