# Product Liability Directive — Council Directive 85/374/EEC

- **CELEX:** `31985L0374`
- **OJ:** L 210, 7.8.1985, p. 29–33
- **Text:** <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:31985L0374>

## Interplay with the AI Act

Cited in AI Act recital 9 (footnote 10 of the OJ text): all consumer rights
and remedies under 85/374/EEC, including compensation for damages caused by
defective products (which can include defective AI systems), remain unaffected
and fully applicable alongside the AI Act.

**Supersession note:** Directive (EU) 2024/2853 (new Product Liability
Directive, CELEX `32024L2853`) modernises the regime and explicitly covers
software and AI; Member States must transpose it by 9 December 2026. For
current analysis prefer 2024/2853; 85/374/EEC is retained here because it is
the reference named in the AI Act's own text.
