# Machinery Regulation — Regulation (EU) 2023/1230

- **CELEX:** `32023R1230`
- **OJ:** L 145, 14.6.2023, p. 185–238
- **Text:** <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023R1230>
- **Application date:** 20 January 2027 (replaces Directive 2006/42/EC)

## Interplay with the AI Act

- Machinery incorporating an AI safety component that undergoes third-party
  conformity assessment falls under **Article 6(1)** high-risk classification
  (Annex I, Section A route); see AI Act recital 50.
- Annex I, Section A of the AI Act lists the Machinery Directive as "Union
  harmonisation legislation" for the embedded high-risk route; the Machinery
  Regulation continues that role post-2027.
- Recital 64 of the AI Act describes complementary application: machinery
  sectoral law covers mechanical risks, the AI Act covers AI-specific risks;
  providers may integrate testing/documentation processes across both regimes.
