# Data Act — Regulation (EU) 2023/2854

- **CELEX:** `32023R2854`
- **OJ:** L, 2023/2854, 22.12.2023
- **Text:** <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32023R2854>
- **Application date:** 12 September 2025

## Interplay with the AI Act

- Cited in the AI Act's footnotes (footnote 43 of the OJ text) among acts not
  affected by the AI Act.
- Relevant to AI training data: connected-product data access/sharing rules
  (Chapters II–III) govern who may access IoT-generated data usable to train
  AI models; the AI Act's GPAI obligations (Art. 53) require a copyright policy
  and training-data summary but do not create data-access rights.
- No direct overlap of obligations: the Data Act governs access to and use of
  industrial (non-personal) data; the AI Act governs AI system/model conduct.
