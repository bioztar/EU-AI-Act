# GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation)

- **CELEX:** `32016R0679`
- **OJ:** L 119, 4.5.2016, p. 1–88
- **Text:** <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679>

## Interplay with the AI Act (Reg (EU) 2024/1689)

Per AI Act recital 10 and Article 2(8) of the Act's final text (see `corpus/act/`):

- The AI Act does **not** affect the application of Union data-protection law,
  including supervisory authorities' tasks and powers.
- Providers/deployers of AI systems remain controllers or processors under the
  GDPR where design, development or use of AI systems involves processing of
  personal data.
- Data subjects retain all GDPR rights, including rights around solely
  automated decision-making and profiling.
- Explicit carve-outs: AI Act Article 10(5) (use of special categories of
  personal data for bias detection/correction) and Article 59 (regulatory
  sandbox data protection derogations).
