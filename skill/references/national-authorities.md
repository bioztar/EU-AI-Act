# National AI Authorities & Transposition Status

The AI Act is a Regulation: it applies directly in every member state — no
national transposition act exists or is required. National-level activity
consists of (1) designating market-surveillance / notifying authorities
(due per Art. 70; contact points published by 2 Aug 2025) and (2) optional
complementary national AI laws. Fundamental-rights authority lists were due
2 Nov 2024 (Art. 77(2)).

**Status legend**: `designated` = official designation verified against an
official source; `reported` = widely reported candidate, not yet verified;
`—` = no confirmed information as of 2026-08-25. **Commission SPOC column** (last verified 2026-10-18): the single point of contact for market surveillance as listed by the European Commission at https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/market-surveillance-authorities-under-ai-act ; `*` = "national designation decision is still pending final adoption" (Commission's wording); `—` = no entry on that list. This list is a primary source for the SPOC only, not for every sectoral MSA. Member States had to designate MSAs by 2 Aug 2025; the Commission states it may launch infringement procedures where they fail to. Verify before relying on any
`reported` entry in client work; enforcement letters should confirm the current
designated authority directly.

Special cases (Art. 74): Annex I product high-risk → sectoral market
surveillance authority; financial institutions → financial supervisor; law
enforcement / border / justice deployments → data protection authority.
Union institutions → EDPS. AI systems built on a provider's own GPAI model, and AI systems in VLOPs/VLOSEs → AI Office (Art. 75(1) as amended by Reg. (EU) 2026/1744, with exceptions). GPAI providers → European Commission / AI Office
exclusively (Art. 88).
