**Ambiguity flagged (quote):** amended Art. 113(c) reads "Chapter III, Sections 1, 2, and 3, with the exception of Article 6(5), shall apply from: (i) 2 December 2027 … Annex III; and (ii) 2 August 2028 … Annex I". Chapter III Section 4 (notifying authorities/notified bodies) still applies from 2 Aug 2025 (Art. 113(b)), and Section 5 (Arts. 40–49: standards, conformity assessment, certificates, registration) is not named in point (c), so on its face falls under the general 2 Aug 2026 date. In practice the Section 5 operator duties (conformity assessment, CE marking, registration) attach to Section 2/3 obligations and to high-risk systems placed on the market under them; we read them as biting together with Sections 1–3, but this is our inference, not text. Re-check against Commission Q&A next pass.

## Grace periods for legacy systems (Art. 111)

- **High-risk systems placed on market/put into service before "the date of application of Chapter III referred to in Article 113"** (i.e. 2 Dec 2027 for Annex III, 2 Aug 2028 for Annex I — per amended Art. 111(2); was a fixed 2 Aug 2026): Regulation applies only if they undergo **significant changes in their designs** as from that date. Otherwise grandfathered — except the public-authority carve-out (2 Aug 2030).
- **Generative AI systems (incl. GPAI systems) placed before 2 Aug 2026**: comply with Art. 50(2) marking by **2 Dec 2026** (Art. 111(4), new).
- **GPAI models placed before 2 Aug 2025**: providers must take necessary steps to comply by **2 Aug 2027** (unchanged, Art. 111(3)).
- Prohibited practices (Art. 5) apply regardless of placement date — no grandfathering (original eight from 2 Feb 2025; points (ba)/(bb) from 2 Dec 2026).

## Innovation-support infrastructure
- National AI regulatory sandboxes: at least one per Member State, operational by **2 Aug 2027** (amended Art. 57(1)); may be joint; Commission technical support. The AI Office may also establish an EU-level sandbox for systems under its Art. 75(1) competence (Art. 57(3a), new) (Arts. 57–59). Sandbox benefits include priority guidance and possible derogations from real-world-testing conditions (Arts. 58–59, 76(2)).
- Testing in real-world conditions regime with informed consent and MSA supervision (Arts. 60–61, 76).
- Real-world testing extended to Annex I Section A systems; new optional framework for Annex I Section B products (Arts. 60, 60a, as amended/new).
- SME support: dedicated priorities in QMS/tech-doc simplification, sandbox access priority, dedicated MSA guidance (Arts. 62–63); SME fines capped lower-of (Art. 99(6)). **SMCs** (small mid-caps, Art. 3(14b), new) now share simplified technical documentation (Art. 11(1)), proportionate QMS (Art. 17(2)) and the lower-of fine cap (Art. 99(6a), new).

## Review calendar (Commission, Art. 112)

- Annually from entry into force: assessment of need to amend Annex III and the Art. 5 prohibition list (112(1)).
- 2 Aug 2028 (+ every 4 years): evaluation incl. Annex III headings, transparency list, supervision/governance effectiveness (112(2)); AI Office functioning review (112(5)); standardisation progress on energy-efficient GPAI development (112(6)).
- 2 Aug 2028 (+ every 3 years): impact/effectiveness of voluntary codes of conduct (112(7)).
- 2 Aug 2029 (+ every 4 years): general evaluation report incl. possible Union agency proposal (112(3)).
- 2 Aug 2031: assessment of enforcement, reporting to Parliament/Council/ECOSOC (112(13)).

Delegated acts sunset/renewal cycle runs in five-year periods from 1 Aug 2024 (Art. 97(2)).

## Practical planning note

A product targeting an Annex III use case must be conformity-assessment-ready by **2 Dec 2027** (Annex I products: 2 Aug 2028); systems placed before those dates are grandfathered unless significantly changed (Art. 111(2), amended). Art. 50 transparency duties already apply (since 2 Aug 2026). The 2025 batch (governance + GPAI) arrived first so that infrastructure (AI Office, notified bodies, database) exists when the main obligations bite. Teams integrating GPAI models downstream inherit compliance-relevant information via Annex XII from 2 Aug 2025 onward.
