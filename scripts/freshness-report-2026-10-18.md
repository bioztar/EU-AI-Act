### 1.1 Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 — "Digital Omnibus on AI" — adopted & in force
- Signed 8 Jul 2026; OJ L, 2026/1744, 24.7.2026; in force **27 Jul 2026** (third day after
  publication). Proposed as COM(2025) 836 on 19 Nov 2025 (Commission). Source: EUR-Lex/CELLAR
  CELEX 32026R1744; Commission news "AI Omnibus enters into force"
  (https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/ai-omnibus-enters-force).
- **The 2026-08-29 pass missed this act**, although it was already in force: its status checks
  said the Annex III high-risk regime applied from 2 Aug 2026. Corrected everywhere.
- Main changes (article refs are to the amended AI Act):
  - Art. 113(c): Annex III high-risk (Ch. III Sec. 1–3) from **2 Dec 2027** (was 2 Aug 2026);
    Annex I from **2 Aug 2028** (was 2 Aug 2027). Art. 113(d) new: Arts. 102–110 from 27 Jul 2026.
  - Art. 5(1)(ba)/(bb) + 5(1a)/(1b) new prohibitions (non-consensual intimate content of
    identifiable persons; CSAM) — apply from **2 Dec 2026** (Art. 113(a)).
  - Art. 4 AI literacy replaced: "take measures to support the development of AI literacy …
    does not require … any specific level". Art. 4a new (special-category data for bias
    detection); Art. 10(5) deleted.
  - Art. 3(14a)/(14b) SME/SMC definitions; SMC relief in Arts. 11(1), 17(2), 99(1), 99(6a)
    (lower-of cap for 99(4)–(5) fines only).
  - Art. 6(1a)–(1c) safety-component clarifications; Annex I Sec. A pt 1 (Machinery Directive)
    deleted, Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 added as Sec. B pt 21.
  - Art. 25(2), (4) value-chain cooperation rewritten; new fine point Art. 99(4)(da).
  - Registration: Annex VIII Sec. B points 7 and 9 deleted (Art. 6(3) systems still register
    under Art. 49(2)).
  - Art. 27 FRIA may reuse DPIA; Art. 40(2), 42(3) (CRA presumption), 43(3) (Annex I Sec. A NBs
    apply for designation by 28 Jan 2028), Annex XIV notified-body codes.
  - Art. 50(7) codes / implementing act; Art. 111(4): pre-2 Aug 2026 generative systems comply
    with Art. 50(2) by **2 Dec 2026**; Art. 111(2) grandfathering now keyed to the Chapter III
    application date; public-authority systems still by 2 Aug 2030.
  - Art. 57(1) national sandboxes by **2 Aug 2027** (was 2 Aug 2026); Art. 57(3a) EU-level
    sandbox; Arts. 60, 60a real-world testing.
  - Art. 72(3) post-market-monitoring guidance + template by **2 Sep 2027** (was implementing
    act by 2 Feb 2026). Art. 96(1)(g) guidelines by **1 Aug 2027**.
  - Art. 75(1) AI Office exclusive competence for AI systems built on the same provider's GPAI
    model (with exceptions) and for AI systems in VLOPs/VLOSEs; Arts. 75a–75d powers, commitments,
    fines, periodic penalty payments; serious incidents for these systems go to the AI Office.
